# SQL-EXCEL-Portfolio
Data Analysis Portfolio

KPI Requirements
Analyzing key indicators for pizza sales data to gain insights into our business performance.

Following metrics can be seen on the porfolio.

Total Revenue: Sum of total price of all pizza orders.
Average Order Value: The average amount spent per order, calculating by dividing total revenue by total number of orders.
Total Pizzas Sold: The sum of quantities of all pizzas sold.
Total Orders: Total number of orders placed.
Average Pizzas Per Orders: The average number of pizzas sold per day, calculated by total number of pizzas sold by total number of orders.

Charts:
Bar Chart: Daily Trend For Total Orders
Line Chart: Hourly Trend For Hourly Orders
Pie Chart: Percentage Sales By Sales Category
Pie Chart: Percentage Sales By Pizza Size
Funnel Chart: Total Pizzas Sold By Total Category
Bar Chart: Top 5 Best Sellers By Total Pizzas Sold
Bar Chart: 5 Worst Sellers By Total Pizzas Sold
